01 Spec sheet

An engine, not a lineup.

Most bookings give a room a soundtrack. Certified_V10 gives it a drivetrain — selectors building pressure track by track, MCs riding the floor's throttle, hosts keeping every table inside the run. The difference between a night you attended and a night that took you somewhere.

Built by and for Bangkok's young nightlife crowd, the unit went from packed afterparties to the city's front-row clubs — and turned an ordinary shift behind the decks into a social event with its own name on the flyer.

Phase 01 · Ignition

Doors open, haze up. The selectors start low and warm the room like oil through an engine — you don't feel the pull until you're already inside it.

The floor fills and the mix tightens. Edits of songs everyone knows, cut a bar before the chorus pays off. Pressure, on purpose.

MCs take the throttle. Confetti, CO2, and the chorus finally lands — the whole room takes the same breath. This is what the unit is certified for.

Engine off, floor still glowing. Slow ones for the diehards, lights half up, everyone deciding where to eat. A good machine cools loudly.

05 Service record

The match ends. The fixture begins.

Once a year the CU–TU match settles the oldest football rivalry in Thailand — and then both sides need the same thing: one floor loud enough to forget the score. That's a Certified shift.

The CU–TU After Party is where the unit made its name, and where half of young Bangkok learned what its logo sounds like. The habit travels — when that crowd lands in Brisbane, Certified lands with them.
