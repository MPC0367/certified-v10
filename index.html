About the collective

Certified_V10 is a DJ collective from Bangkok that has been throwing parties since 2023. Four DJs share one booth, and one MC works the room.

The sound moves between house, hip-hop and the Thai anthems a Bangkok floor knows by heart.

An engine, not a lineup: five people, one night, played as a unit.

How a night runs

Four phases, from doors to close. Select a phase.

1 Ignition

Around 22:30

118 BPM indicative tempo

90160

Doors open. The DJs start low and warm the room, so the floor fills before anyone notices the pull.

2 Build

Around 00:00

128 BPM indicative tempo

90160

The floor fills and the mix tightens. Songs everyone knows, held back a bar before the chorus.

3 Redline

Around 01:30

150 BPM indicative tempo

90160

The MC takes the room. The chorus lands and the whole floor moves together. This is the peak of the night.

4 Overrun

Around 03:30

100 BPM indicative tempo

90160

The tempo drops. Slower songs for the people who stayed, lights half up, the night winding down.
